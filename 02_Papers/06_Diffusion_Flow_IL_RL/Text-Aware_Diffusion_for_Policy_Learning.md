## Research Summary（研究摘要）

- **Problem:** 研究主题为 `Text-conditioned Reward`；本卡仅按官方摘要级证据整理，未替代正文精读。
- **Main Contribution:**
  - To address this challenge, we propose Text-Aware Diffusion for Policy Learning (TADPoLe), which uses a pretrained, frozen text-conditioned diffusion model to compute dense zero-shot reward signals for text-aligned policy learning.
  - In our experiments, we demonstrate that TADPoLe is able to learn policies for novel goal-achievement and continuous locomotion behaviors specified by natural language, in both Humanoid and Dog environments.
- **Key Idea:** 以 `Text-conditioned Reward` 为主要方法/能力线索；更细的结构和消融结论待正文核验。
